# FinalApp privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 (no Accessibility service)

FinalApp helps you scroll less on social media apps. This page explains what data it uses.

## What stays on your phone
- How long you use the apps you choose (read from Android's usage access).
- When an app you chose opens (read from the same usage access).
- Your settings, limits, history and Daily Proof.

None of this leaves your phone, except in the league described below.

## Display over other apps
FinalApp uses this permission only to show its pause screen and timer on top of an app you chose to limit. It does not read anything on your screen.

## League (optional)
If you start or join a league, FinalApp sends your chosen league name and your minutes per day to Google Firebase, so the people in your league can see them. Nothing else is sent. You get a random anonymous account; no email, phone number or real name is needed. Leaving the league stops the updates.

## Daily Proof
If you add a friend, FinalApp opens a ready message to them in your own messaging app. You decide whether to send it. FinalApp does not send messages itself.

## What we never do
We never sell, share or use your data for advertising or anything else. There are no ads and no trackers.
